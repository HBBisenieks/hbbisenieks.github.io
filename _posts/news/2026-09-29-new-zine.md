folks who follow my socials, such as they are, may have seen by now that i've been working on 
a new zine about archery. but for folks who may not have seen, my zine [a tgirl's guide to archery](https://hbbisenieks.itch.io/a-tgirls-guide-to-archery) 
is available for $5 and up *right now* on itch.io!

whether or not you're trans or a girl (or even if you're cishet), if you've been interested 
in archery but didn't know where to start, this 22-page zine is full of advice, hand-drawn 
illustrations, diagrams, and maybe humor designed to get you into the sport.
